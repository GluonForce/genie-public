Summary

An approval tracker for supplier invoices. Team members submit invoices, managers approve or reject them, and finance sees what is ready to pay.

Intent

Stop approving invoices over email. Give managers one queue and give finance a clear list of what is ready to pay.

Functional requirements

  1. 1Show a list of invoices with supplier, amount, due date and status.
  2. 2Let a team member add an invoice with supplier, amount, due date and a note.
  3. 3Let a manager approve or reject an invoice and leave a comment.
  4. 4Give finance a "ready to pay" view that lists approved invoices.
  5. 5Filter the list by status and by supplier.
  6. 6Highlight invoices that are due within 7 days.

User stories

  • As a team member, I want to submit an invoice so that it does not get lost in email.
  • As a manager, I want to see what is waiting for me so that nothing is paid late.
  • As a manager, I want to reject an invoice with a comment so that the submitter knows what to fix.
  • As finance, I want a list of approved invoices so that I know what to pay.
