What you say

"Our team approves supplier invoices over email and they get lost. I want one place where a manager approves or rejects each invoice, and finance sees what is ready to pay."

What Genie writes

Supplier invoice approvals
  • Team members submit an invoice with supplier, amount and due date
  • Managers approve or reject each invoice with a comment
  • Finance sees a list of approved invoices that are ready to pay

What a Genie prototype is

A real web app you can click through and show to other people. It is made to test an idea, so it looks and behaves like the product without the parts that take months to build.

Works in the browser

Screens, forms and navigation all work. Open it on a laptop or a phone.

A link to share

Anyone with the link can open it. It is not listed anywhere and search engines do not index it.

Data stays on the device

The prototype imitates a backend. What you type is saved in your own browser and is not sent to a server.

Not a finished product

There are no real accounts, payments or connections to other services. Web only for now.

Questions

Why does a person review my requirements?

So that nothing is built that is unclear, out of scope or abusive. The reviewer can only accept or decline. Your requirements stay exactly as you approved them.

What if the requirements are not right?

Tell Genie what to change and it rewrites them. You can do this twice. After that you either approve them or decline. Declining uses up your free prototype.

Who can see my prototype?

Anyone you give the link to. The link is hard to guess and is not listed publicly. We can take a prototype offline if it breaks our rules.

What happens after 3 days?

The link stops working. We keep the prototype, so it can be put back online once that feature is released.
